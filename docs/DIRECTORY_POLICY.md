# Directory rules

- **Official versus community:** atlasportal links only to destinations verified as official through first-party sources. starportal contains community works. Both indexes themselves are unofficial; Discord presence alone does not prove official status or endorsement.
- **Identity before individual posts:** use a project's canonical homepage, a creator's main YouTube channel or main social account. Individual videos, posts, playlists and articles can support relevance but are not creator entries.
- **One identity:** avoid duplicates. A YouTube creator belongs to one primary class: reporting/gameplay (`video`), narrative (`series`), or original music (`music`). Using someone else's song in a video does not establish an original music project.
- **Relevance and evidence:** supply public evidence of substantial Star Atlas relevance. Describe what exists without promising outcomes. Declare self-submissions; the maintainer's projects follow the same criteria.
- **Verification:** retain each entry's actual `checkedAt` date and concise public source. `meta.updated` describes the directory revision, not a blanket recheck. A destination without a confirmed URL has an empty URL and `verified: false`.
- **Activity:** YouTube core/inner/outer tiers describe recency of Star Atlas content, not quality, popularity, official approval or payment. Recent original work, update dates and frequency inform ordering. Historical creators can remain in outer tiers. Evidence can be corrected through an issue.
- **Corrections and removal:** creators may request correction or removal. Include a public ownership signal or arrange private verification without posting personal information. Dead or suspicious links are reviewed before promotion; no pay-for-placement or preferential review for the maintainer's work.

## 收錄原則摘要

官方目的地與社群作品分開；以專案首頁和創作者主帳號為入口，同一身分不重複。作者可提交自己的作品，但須揭露關係並適用相同標準。活躍層級只表示 Star Atlas 內容近期性，不表示品質或人氣。核對日期逐筆保存，修正與移除可透過 Issue 提出。
